Photo Bubble Design Approaches

Three ways to handle the photo marker bubble on the map

A

Minimal Bubble — Image Only

48px circular thumbnail on map. Click → expands to 120px rounded bubble with narrow border, small triangle pointer pointing down at the marker. No text inside bubble, date shown below the bubble. Delete button: small circular transparent button at bottom-right of bubble, trash icon, opaque on hover.

B

Rich Bubble — Image + Caption Preview

Same as A but bubble shows 2-line caption preview inside. Date at bottom. More info density but busier.

Hover Tooltip — No Click Needed

Hover over marker shows bubble, click marker jumps directly to lightbox. Faster but less discoverable.

Lightbox Layout

All options use the same lightbox: centered overlay, darkened backdrop, click outside to close. Inside: left 65% image, right 35% info panel (uploader avatar + name, location name, description, date). Warm russet/terracotta accent colors.
